Clipstack
Video Editor for iPhone

Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Short version: Clipstack doesn't collect any of your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your videos, voice recordings and captions stay on your iPhone unless you choose to export, share or back them up.

Who we are

Clipstack is made by Ryan Tag, an independent developer. If you have a question about privacy, email hello@ryantagcoffee.com.

What Clipstack collects

Nothing. Clipstack has no servers of its own and sends nothing about you or your projects to the developer or to anyone else. It doesn't include analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it doesn't use any third-party services that collect data.

Your videos and photos

When you add clips from your photo library, Clipstack copies them into its own storage on your iPhone so your projects keep working even if you later delete the originals. You choose which items to add; Clipstack can't see the rest of your library.

When you export, Clipstack asks for permission to add the finished video to your photo library. This permission only lets it save new videos; it can't read or change anything already there.

Microphone

Clipstack uses the microphone only while you record a voiceover. Recordings are saved inside your project on your iPhone.

Captions

Captions are made on your iPhone using Apple's built-in speech recognition. Your audio isn't sent to the developer. The first time you make captions in a language, iOS may download that language's speech model from Apple. Apple's privacy policy covers that download.

Backups

Backup is optional and off until you turn it on. You choose the folder, for example in iCloud Drive or on a USB drive, and Clipstack copies your projects there. Whoever provides that folder, such as Apple for iCloud Drive, handles your files under its own privacy policy. The developer has no access to them.

Feedback

If you tap Send feedback, your email app opens a message to the developer. We only receive what you choose to send, use it to reply and improve the app, and never sell or share it.

Children

Clipstack doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Everything Clipstack stores is on your iPhone. Delete a project in the app to remove it and its imported media, or delete the app to remove everything. Backups you made stay in the folder you chose until you delete them there. Videos you exported stay in your photo library.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
