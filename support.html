Common questions

What do I need to run Clipstack?

An iPhone with iOS 26 or later. On iPhone Duo, Clipstack also adapts to the open screen, the half-folded tabletop pose and a side bar of buttons you choose.

Where are my projects stored?

On your iPhone, inside Clipstack. Clips you add are copied in, so a project keeps working even if you delete the original from Photos. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

How do I back up or move my projects?

In Clipstack's Settings, tap Backup, then Back up to a folder… and choose a folder, such as one in iCloud Drive. Clipstack copies your projects there. To bring one back, tap Restore from backup… on the same page and pick the project's folder.

Where do exported videos go?

To your photo library. The first time, iOS asks whether Clipstack may add videos to Photos. If you said no, you can change it in the Settings app → Clipstack → Photos.

Voiceover won't record

Clipstack needs microphone access. Turn it on in the Settings app → Clipstack → Microphone.

Captions aren't appearing

Captions use Apple's on-device speech recognition. The first time, iOS may need to download a language model, so keep your iPhone online for a moment. Captions work best with clear speech. Add names and brands to Words to know so they're spelled the way you want.

Clipstack is using a lot of storage

Projects hold copies of your clips, plus smaller copies used for smooth editing. Long 4K videos take a lot of space. On the projects screen, press and hold a project and choose Free up space…, or delete projects you've finished and exported.

How do I delete a project?

On the projects screen, press and hold a project, then choose Delete. This removes its imported media from Clipstack. Your Photos library isn't touched.
